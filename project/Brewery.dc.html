Flagships

“Bloody Butcher” Cream Ale
Cream Ale · 5.0% ABV · 18 IBU
Nearly 300 pounds of our heirloom Bloody Butcher corn give it a creamy mouthfeel and gentle sweetness, finished with fresh tangerine and Amarillo hops.
Best of Loudoun Beer 2025
Silver · 2025 Brewers’ Choice
Bronze · 2024 VA Craft Beer Cup
“Pilots” Pilsner
German Pilsner · 5.2% ABV · 12 IBU
Pilsner malt and lager yeast fermented warm, balanced with Hallertau and Tettnang hops. The easy-drinking go-to.
“Guns A Go-Go” West Coast IPA
West Coast IPA · 5.4% ABV · 50 IBU
Chinook, Centennial and Simcoe for dank citrus, fermented with yeast from Jasper Yeast Labs in Dulles, VA.
“Military Hop” New England IPA
NEIPA · 6.0% ABV · 25.5 IBU
Amarillo, Mosaic, Lotus and Trident, then dry hopped with Mosaic, Lotus and Simcoe. Unfiltered and tropical.
“Wilco Wit” Witbier
Wheat Ale · 5.4% ABV · 18 IBU
Sweet orange peel, coriander and grains of paradise on a Winter White Wheat base. An all-season drinker.
