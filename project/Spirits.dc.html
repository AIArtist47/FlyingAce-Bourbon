Farm-to-bottle

From our fields to your glass

Dark red in color and bold in flavor, our heirloom Bloody Butcher corn is grown, harvested and milled on site. Crafted with malted barley and wheat, it gives our whiskey a grain-forward nose with undertones of banana bread and kettle corn.

01
Grow
Heirloom Bloody Butcher corn planted on the farm.
02
Harvest
Picked from the fields that surround the distillery.
03
Store & Mill
Stored and milled on the property.
04
Distill
Paired with malted barley and wheat and run off our still.
05
Bottle
Straight off the still as White Dog - or aged into bourbon.
