Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is Flying Ace Farm hiring?
Yes, we’d love to chat! Apply online through our Apply Now form.
What food options do you have?
Monk’s on the Farm is our on-site restaurant serving BBQ, including smashburgers, veggie burgers, fried chicken and seasonal salads. Weekly specials include Kids Eat Free and a food special every Wednesday, a Burger of the Week every Thursday, and Friday specials.
Do you allow outside food?
No outside food or beverages are allowed, with the exception of birthday cakes.
Do you take reservations?
No reservations are required - we have plenty of indoor and outdoor space. For small and large group table reservations, email events@flyingacefarm.com.
Do you allow dogs?
Yes, we’re pet friendly as long as your pet is friendly! Outside only and leashed at all times.
Do you have live music? How do I sign my band up?
Yes. Please complete our Live Music Inquiries form.
Do you host private events?
Yes, contact us through our event inquiry form and we’d be happy to assist.
Do you host weddings?
Yes, email us at weddings@flyingacefarm.com.
Do you offer gift cards?
Yes, gift cards are available to purchase online on our Gift Cards page.
Can I take beer into the distillery, or cocktails and flights outside their areas?
Our Distillery Tasting Room & ABC Store allows up to 2 cocktails and is zoned separately under VA ABC law. Restaurant cocktails, beer and wine can be enjoyed throughout the rest of the property. Any ABC manager on site is happy to help.
We’re a working farm

Farm rules

A few ground rules keep the farm safe and fun for everyone.

01No outside food or drink (birthday cakes excepted)
02No coolers, backpacks or bags other than a purse or diaper bag
03No tents, drones or RC devices
04No sports equipment - balls, bats, frisbees, etc.
05Children must be supervised at all times; not permitted in the 21+ area
06Pets leashed at all times, outdoor areas only
07Parking is for patrons only; no overnight parking
08No loitering or soliciting
09We’re a working farm: please stay out of the pond, creeks, cornfields and production areas
10Have fun!
